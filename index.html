How to make a ranking video

  1. 1Pick your list sizeChoose Top 3 up to Top 10 - every rank gets its own numbered slot.
  2. 2Add one clip per rankUpload a video into each slot, rename its label and pick its text colour. Use Add multiple clips to fill empty slots in one go.
  3. 3Trim each clipDrag the start and end sliders so only the moment that matters plays. Set clip volume, or mute a clip, per slot.
  4. 4Choose the playback orderCountdown plays Top N down to number 1, ascending plays 1 up, and random shuffles a fresh mix you can preview before exporting.
  5. 5Style the titlePick one of 32 text presets or build your own look - font, outline, shadow, box, highlight word and alignment are all editable.
  6. 6Set the canvas9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, 4:5 for portrait posts, 1:1 for square. Add a local music file if you want a backing track.
  7. 7ExportDownload an MP4 or a WebM - both include your clip sound and background music on supported browsers, rendered entirely on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ranking Studio Web really free?

Yes. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so there is nothing to sign up for and no watermark on exports.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Files you pick are read locally by your browser and rendered on your device. Closing the tab removes them; project files store only names, labels and trim settings.

MP4 or WebM - which should I export?

MP4 (via WebCodecs, in Chrome and Edge) is rendered frame-by-frame and includes clip sound plus your background music, mixed and encoded as AAC audio in the same file. A browser that cannot encode AAC will still export the MP4, but without sound - the export result tells you when that happens, and in that case WebM is the pick for audio: it is recorded in realtime and also includes clip sound plus your background music.

Which video formats can I add?

Anything your browser can play - MP4 with H.264 is the safest choice, and WebM and most MOV files work too. If a file will not preview, convert it to H.264 MP4 first.

Why does a reopened project ask me to re-link files?

Browsers cannot keep access to files on your disk, and this tool does not upload or store your media. The project file keeps every setting, label and trim - you just point each slot back at its file.
